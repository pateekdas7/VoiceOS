### Design Decisions

- **LoA checker skips negotiation offer turns** (`negotiation_offer_inr is not None`): agent offers are operational actions (handled by NegotiationEnvelopeChecker), not factual claims about the account. This prevents double-counting and aligns with RI-5 intent.
- **Lazy pydantic imports**: `IntentEngine`/`TurnInput`/`TurnRole`/`UtteranceSegment` imported inside method bodies so the checker classes work without pydantic v2 installed.
- **Dependency injection**: `IntentAccuracyEvaluator(model=...)` and `FounderValidationSuite(intent_evaluator=...)` for deterministic unit testing.
